Privacy Policy

How we handle cookies and advertising tracking on this website. Last updated 14 October 2026.

Who we are

This website, pillarscoffee.sg, is operated by Pillars Coffee, a specialty coffee business based at 1 Fusionopolis Walk, #01-K1, Solaris, Singapore 138628. For any privacy question, email hello@pillarscoffee.sg.

Cookies and advertising tracking

With your consent, we use the Meta Pixel (provided by Meta Platforms, Inc.) on this website. The Meta Pixel places a cookie in your browser and sends Meta information about your visit, such as the pages you view and actions you take. We use this to measure the performance of our advertising and to show you relevant ads on Meta platforms such as Facebook and Instagram (this is commonly called retargeting).

We also store a small record of your consent choice in your browser so that we do not ask you again on every visit.

Your choices and how to opt out

You can turn off advertising cookies at any time using the Cookie Settings link in the footer of any page. When you opt out, the Meta Pixel stops loading on future page views in that browser. You may also clear cookies and site data in your browser, or use your browser's Do Not Track or ad-blocking settings.

What data is collected

When tracking is enabled, Meta may receive your IP address, browser and device information, the pages you visit on this site, and a cookie identifier. We do not sell your personal data. We do not collect special categories of personal data through this website.

How Meta uses the data

Meta processes this information as a data controller for its own purposes, including ad delivery and measurement, as described in Meta's own Data Policy at facebook.com/privacy/policy.

Payments and orders

When you place an order, we collect the details you provide (name, email, phone, and, for delivery, your address) to fulfil your order. Payments are processed securely by HitPay; we do not store your card details. This information is used only to process and deliver your order.

Your rights

Under Singapore's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA), you may request access to, or correction of, the personal data we hold about you, and you may withdraw consent for its use. To make a request, email hello@pillarscoffee.sg.
